The Innovation

The PUSH. Lip Balm utilizes a proprietary mechanical design to ensure zero waste.

  • Lid: Designed with a rod on the side used to push the remaining product.
  • Base Disc: Ensures that all product is pushed up evenly, rather than just the center.
  • Elevator: Holds the lip balm and is weaved along the spirals of the external tube.
  • External Tube: Features spiraled grooves on the inside for smooth elevation.
  • Internal Tube: Contains the locking mechanism for top/bottom and facilitates turning the balm up and down.

Pricing Strategy & Lifecycle

Phase 1: Initial Launch

Penetration Pricing: 20% sale/discount for the first 3 months.

  • Pros: Quickly grabs market share; encourages buyers to try our unique design.
  • Cons: Results in smaller profit margins initially; high production costs risk early negative cash flow.

Phase 2: When Established

Competitive Pricing

  • Pros: Differentiates brand through USP rather than price; retains loyal customers despite small price increases.
  • Cons: Must rely heavily on non-price ways to attract sales; monitoring competitor prices is time-consuming.
